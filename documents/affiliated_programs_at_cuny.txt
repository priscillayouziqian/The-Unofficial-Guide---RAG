Topic: CUNY Study Abroad and Affiliated Programs

CUNY Study Abroad?
Has anyone here done a cuny study abroad program? how was it? where did you go? how much did it cost? how did housing work? so curious

i looked into it, but ultimately chose not to do it as i didn't like the location options in relation to my major.

there are different types of study abroad options, the ones i looked into were "affliated" which is a program taught at the foreign school by their teachers. the other types include ones taught by cuny professors and of course, the foreign student exchange program. how much it costs depends on the location and how long you're staying. could be a summer, semester or year long thing. housing is typically on you but isn't too expensive(especially compared to NY). there are scholarships you can apply for. i think some financial aid might cover it, but i wasn't eligible for financial aid so i didn't ask about that. there should be a study abroad representative at your school, they could tell you more.

As for the biggest challenge with getting study abroad credits to transfer: from my experience with CUNY affiliated programs, the most common challenge is getting specific foreign courses pre-approved by academic department advisors. You have to do this to ensure they fulfill major requirements rather than just counting as general electives. It can be a bureaucratic headache!
