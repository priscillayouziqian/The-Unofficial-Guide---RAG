I had the opportunity to do a winter sem abroad through Queens college in Barcelona! It was expensive but a lot of fun!! For Queens College I believe you just need to fill out the application on time but there is no essay or interview. I also believe QC is one of the CUNYs with a wide variety of study abroad programs. My study abroad was faculty led so there was no language barrier. My dorming experience was okay and normal - hated how cold it was in Barca though but I had a kitchen and bathroom in my room (there was two beds in one room so I had a roomate).
